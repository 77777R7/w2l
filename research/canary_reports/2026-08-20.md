## Reading the numbers

- bare-http sends no user-agent and no extraction: its blocked/failed counts are the open-web floor, not a regression.
- golden/extract-tf/resilient share the polite UA; differences among them are extraction quality, not politeness.
- Tier 1 is expected to clear on the http lane alone. Tier 2 is NOT: every tier-2 failure mode (bot gate, auth wall, JS shell) is precisely what the browser lane exists to solve — the tier-2 contentful rate IS the browser-lane value case, measured.
- browser-local is the escalation target the http arms flag into: same extract-tf cascade, real Chromium. Its tier-2 delta against resilient-http is the measured value of the browser lane.
- challenge_text_returned failures on real pages double as bot-gate measurements.
