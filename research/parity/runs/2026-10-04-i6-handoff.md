## What each failure was

- **Run 1.** The person was through the page ("You bypassed the Cloudflare challenge!"), but W2L still read the page as `cloudflare_challenge`.
  - Cause: the gate took Cloudflare's bot-management script, which Cloudflare injects into a site's ordinary pages (`/cdn-cgi/challenge-platform/scripts/jsd/main.js`), for the challenge's own plumbing.
  - Fixed in `d821f88` for every lane.
- **Run 2.** Chrome refused the connection: Allow was not clicked, or remote debugging was off. The person turned it back on.
- **Run 3.** Again the page was not read, and still read as a check ("shows a captcha"). The run was stopped with Ctrl-C, so it gave no reason. **Unconfirmed:** we took the cause to be the Turnstile script the page keeps once passed, and gated a page with content by decisive evidence alone, as the lanes do (`fed91fb`). From run 4 on, W2L judged the page through ("shows no check in your Chrome").
- **Runs 4 and 5, cloudflare-challenge.** W2L asked for the click on the page ("shows no check in your Chrome: click on the page"), but no user activation was seen on the tab's document.
  - A read-only check of the tab during run 4 found two scrapingcourse tabs open, one left over from run 3, both in the background, with no activation on either.
  - The person reports they clicked.
  - In `dd947e9` W2L brings its tab to the front and reads twice a second.
  - In run 6 a read-only watcher (a second CDP connection) logged the tab's state each second (times below are UTC). It saw the click on antibot-challenge (`hasBeenActive` true at 03:52:13) and on nowsecure.nl (true at 03:52:20). cloudflare-challenge was read and its tab closed at about 03:52:10, between two of the watcher's reads.
  - **Unconfirmed:** why the clicks of runs 4 and 5 were not seen. The two most likely explanations are a click in the wrong tab and a click on the browser's own UI, but neither is proven.
- **nowsecure.nl, every run.** The page is a Turnstile widget demo (sitekey `3x00000000000000000000FF`) with no content behind it: its text is "NOWSECURE by nodriver".
  - The watcher's snapshot of the page the person saw after passing still holds the widget (`div.cf-turnstile` with `data-sitekey`) and its script.
  - With no content beside the widget, W2L's gate reads it as a captcha. Had it been read, it would have been `failed` (`empty_unverified`), which replaces nothing.
  - We count it as a page W2L could not read, not as a success.
